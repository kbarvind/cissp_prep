- **Patents and trade secrets** can both protect intellectual property related to a manufacturing process. 
- **Trade secrets** are appropriate only when the details can be tightly controlled within an organization, so a patent is the appropriate solution in this case
- **Copyrights** are used to protect creative works
- **Trademarks** are used to protect names, logos, and symbols.
